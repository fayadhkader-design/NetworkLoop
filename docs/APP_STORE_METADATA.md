## Description

NetworkLoop is a simple personal CRM built for students and young professionals navigating
networking, recruiting, and interviews.

Keep the important context from every professional relationship in one private place:

- Save contacts with their company, role, industry, and status
- Record networking calls, interviews, coffee chats, recruiter emails, and follow-ups
- Remember what you discussed and capture the next step
- Schedule follow-up dates and see what is due or overdue
- Search contacts by name, company, or role
- Track your networking activity from a focused dashboard

NetworkLoop is intentionally lightweight. It gives you enough structure to stay thoughtful and
consistent without turning relationship-building into a complicated sales workflow.

Your account data is protected with per-user access controls, and you can permanently delete your
account and stored CRM data from Settings at any time.

## Review notes

NetworkLoop is a personal CRM for professional networking. Users create an email/password account,
confirm their email, and can add private contacts and conversation notes.

Please use the review account supplied in App Store Connect. It includes sample contacts and
conversation history so every core screen can be evaluated.

Account deletion is available at Settings → Delete account. The user must type DELETE and confirm
the final system dialog. This permanently deletes the authentication account and cascades deletion
to the profile, contacts, and conversations.

No paid content, advertising, location access, camera access, contact-book access, health data, or
financial transactions are included in version 1.0.
