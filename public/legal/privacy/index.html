NETWORKLOOP

Privacy Policy

Effective June 21, 2026

What NetworkLoop stores

NetworkLoop stores your account email and name, plus the professional contacts, notes, conversation history, and follow-up dates you choose to enter. You should only add information you are permitted to keep.

How information is used

Your information is used only to provide authentication and personal CRM features. NetworkLoop does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Storage and security

Account and CRM data are stored using Supabase. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, and row-level access controls restrict users to their own records.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you remove it or delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently removes your authentication account, contacts, and conversation notes.

Professional contact information

You are responsible for entering and using information about professional contacts lawfully, respectfully, and only for legitimate personal networking or recruiting purposes.

Children

NetworkLoop is intended for people aged 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13.

Questions

Visit the NetworkLoop support page for privacy questions or data requests.
